# Arabic review pack for the native editor

The Arabic on this site was written by the build team, not by a native copywriter. This pack tells the reviewer what to check and which choices were made.
Full Arabic text: `CONTENT-AR.md` (regenerate with `npm run content`). Edit the copy in `src/*.mjs`, not in the Markdown.

## Register and style
- Formal modern standard Arabic suited to Gulf business readers; second person singular masculine for "you" (a neutral-gender form is not used). Please confirm, or switch to plural or feminine-inclusive forms where preferred.
- Western digits (0–9) are used throughout, for consistency with Saudi business documents and invoices. Change globally if you prefer Eastern digits.
- Phone numbers, emails and the licence number are wrapped as left-to-right.

## Things a native reader should look at first
1. Home hero and the three words under it (وضوح / ثقة / علاقات).
2. The Privacy Notice and Terms: legal Arabic should be checked by the same counsel who reviews the English.
3. The scorecard statements: they are rated 0–3, so grammar of each statement must read naturally with the answer scale.
4. The team bios and client quotes (translated from the English published by the firm).
5. Right-to-left layout of tables, the basket and the invoice.
