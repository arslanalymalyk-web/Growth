Concept: a pointed gateway arch drawn as the letter G. Three rising bars and a gold node sit inside it, over a drafting rule. Gateway + growth + blueprint.

Type

Wordmark: Newsreader Medium, capitals, 13% tracking. Descriptor: Inter SemiBold, 36% tracking. Arabic: Noto Naskh Arabic Bold. All lettering in the SVG files is outlined, so no fonts are needed to use them.

Usage

Keep clear space equal to the height of the arch's crossbar on all sides. Minimum size: 24 px for the mark, 120 px wide for the horizontal lockup. Use the reversed lockup on navy or photography, one-colour versions for stamps and single-ink print. Do not stretch, recolour, outline or place the mark on busy imagery without a navy overlay.
