Growth Blueprint — logo suite

Concept: a pointed gateway arch (Najdi-inspired, not a copy of any building) drawn as the letter G. Three rising bars and a gold node sit inside it, over a drafting rule. Gateway + growth + blueprint.

Primary lockup

Stacked · Horizontal · Mark · App icon

Small sizes and alternative

Use the small mark below 32 px (favicons, social avatars, app badges). It drops the gold node and the drafting rule and thickens the strokes.

Alternative B replaces the three bars with one rising step line. It is less generic than a bar chart; choose it if the bars feel too familiar.

Print colour (approximate, confirm against a physical swatch book)

Navy: Pantone 2767 C or 5395 C · Gold: Pantone 7562 C, or metallic foil 871 C for letterheads and cards · Ivory paper: any warm white uncoated stock. CMYK navy about 100/85/40/55; CMYK gold about 25/35/70/5. Always proof before a full run.

Type

Wordmark: Newsreader Medium, capitals, 13% tracking. Descriptor: Inter SemiBold, 36% tracking. Arabic: Noto Naskh Arabic Bold. All lettering in the SVG files is outlined, so no fonts are needed to use them.

Usage

Keep clear space on all sides equal to the width of one bar plus its gap (about one-tenth of the mark's height). Minimum size: 24 px for the mark, 120 px wide for the horizontal lockup. Use the reversed lockup on navy or photography, one-colour versions for stamps and single-ink print. Do not stretch, recolour, outline or place the mark on busy imagery without a navy overlay.
