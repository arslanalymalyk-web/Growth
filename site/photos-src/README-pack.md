# Growth Blueprint image source pack

Contains three Saudi hero scenes and 13 distinct industry photos, plus an English/Arabic image credits page and machine-readable CSV/JSON credits. All 16 assets were checked against Wikimedia Commons file metadata and use CC BY licenses (no CC BY-SA, NC, or ND files).

## Use
- Copy `images/` into your site's public asset directory.
- Preview `image-credits.html` locally or publish it as `/image-credits`.
- Keep `image-credits.csv` or `manifest.json` with the project for auditing.
- The source page and license URLs must remain accessible from your published credits page.

The best available reusable Riyadh image found under the requested licenses shows the skyline at night rather than blue hour; the selected image is credited accurately. The industry photos are globally sourced illustrations, not evidence of Saudi client facilities. Asset files were resized to a maximum width of 1800 px for web use; no crops or color edits were made.
